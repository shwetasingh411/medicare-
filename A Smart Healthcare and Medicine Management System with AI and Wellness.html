Medi Care+
A Smart Healthcare and Medicine Management System with AI and Wellness Integration
When Technology Meets Healthcare
In today's fast-paced world, patients frequently forget to take medications on time—creating serious health risks. Elderly individuals face even greater challenges requiring constant monitoring.
Medi Care+ bridges this gap by combining modern technology, AI, and healthcare into one intelligent platform.
Our Mission
Timely Medicine Intake
Smart reminders ensure medications are taken on schedule
Reduce Health Risks
Prevent complications from missed doses
Digital Medical Records
Secure storage of complete patient history
Caregiver Integration
Real-time alerts for family and healthcare providers
Key Features
Smart Medicine Reminder
Mandatory confirmation ensures accountability and tracks actual intake
Elderly Care Support
Caregiver notifications and integrated monitoring for vulnerable users
Medical History Storage
Comprehensive digital records accessible to authorized healthcare providers
AI Chatbot Assistant
Intelligent 24/7 support for medication queries and wellness guidance
Ayurveda Integration
Traditional Indian wellness practices including yoga and pranayama
Advanced User Experience
Health Dashboard
Medicines taken, streak tracking, and wellness metrics at a glance
User Profile Page
Complete personal details with caregiver contact information
Medicine Schedule
Table view showing name, dosage, time, and intake status
Progress Tracker
Weekly calendar visualizing medication adherence patterns
Dark Mode
Comfortable viewing with toggle between light and dark interfaces
